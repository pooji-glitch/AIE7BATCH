# Tesla Investment Tracker - Data Files

This directory contains comprehensive datasets for analyzing Tesla (TSLA) as an investment opportunity. The data includes research papers, product reviews, market sentiment, financial metrics, and competitor analysis.

## Data Files Overview

### 1. `tesla_research_papers.csv`
**Content**: Academic and industry research papers analyzing Tesla's business, technology, and market position
**Key Fields**:
- `paper_id`: Unique identifier for each research paper
- `title`: Paper title
- `authors`: Research authors
- `publication_year`: Year of publication
- `journal`: Publication source
- `abstract`: Paper summary
- `tesla_products_mentioned`: Tesla products discussed
- `key_findings`: Main conclusions
- `sentiment`: Overall sentiment (Positive/Neutral/Negative)
- `investment_implications`: Investment recommendations

**Use Cases**: Academic research analysis, technology trend assessment, investment thesis development

### 2. `tesla_product_reviews.json`
**Content**: Detailed product reviews and ratings for Tesla vehicles and energy products
**Key Fields**:
- Product information (name, category, price, release year)
- Multiple reviews per product with ratings and detailed feedback
- Pros and cons analysis
- Investment ratings (Strong Buy/Buy/Hold/Sell)
- Market position and competitive advantages

**Use Cases**: Product quality assessment, customer satisfaction analysis, competitive positioning

### 3. `market_sentiment_data.csv`
**Content**: Daily market sentiment data from various sources
**Key Fields**:
- `date`: Date of sentiment measurement
- `source`: Data source (Twitter, Reddit, Analyst Reports, etc.)
- `sentiment_score`: Numerical sentiment score (0-1)
- `confidence`: Confidence level in sentiment analysis
- `volume`: Number of mentions
- `positive_mentions`, `negative_mentions`, `neutral_mentions`: Breakdown
- `key_topics`: Main discussion topics
- `market_impact`: Impact assessment

**Use Cases**: Social sentiment analysis, market mood tracking, trend identification

### 4. `tesla_financial_metrics.csv`
**Content**: Quarterly financial performance data for Tesla
**Key Fields**:
- Revenue, margins, net income
- Vehicle deliveries
- Free cash flow
- R&D expenses
- Financial ratios (P/E, debt-to-equity)
- Market capitalization

**Use Cases**: Financial analysis, performance tracking, valuation modeling

### 5. `competitor_analysis.csv`
**Content**: Comparative analysis of Tesla vs. competitors
**Key Fields**:
- Company information and ticker symbols
- Financial metrics comparison
- EV delivery volumes
- Analyst ratings and price targets
- Competitive advantages and weaknesses

**Use Cases**: Competitive analysis, market positioning, investment comparison

## Data Quality Notes

- **Research Papers**: 15 papers spanning 2022-2023 with focus on Tesla's technology, market position, and investment potential
- **Product Reviews**: 5 Tesla products with multiple reviews from reputable sources
- **Market Sentiment**: 20 days of sentiment data from multiple sources
- **Financial Metrics**: 20 quarters of historical data (2019-2023)
- **Competitor Analysis**: 15 major automotive and EV companies

## Usage Guidelines

1. **Data Integration**: Use these datasets together for comprehensive Tesla investment analysis
2. **Sentiment Analysis**: Combine research papers and market sentiment for trend analysis
3. **Financial Modeling**: Use financial metrics for valuation and performance analysis
4. **Competitive Intelligence**: Compare Tesla's position against competitors
5. **Product Analysis**: Assess Tesla's product portfolio strength and customer satisfaction

- **Technical Analysis**: Combine sentiment data with financial performance
